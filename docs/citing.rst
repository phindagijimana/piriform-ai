Citing
======

If you use ideas or code from this project, please cite the underlying
methods, especially:

* The PC labelling protocol and labels: :ref:`Steinbart et al. 2023 <ref-steinbart>`.
* nnU-Net: :ref:`Isensee et al. 2021 <ref-isensee>`.
* SynthSeg: :ref:`Billot et al. 2023 <ref-billot>`.
* The crop / topology / epoch-selection recipe: :ref:`Mauri et al. 2025 <ref-mauri>`.
* Multi-atlas baselines: :ref:`Heckemann et al. 2010 <ref-heckemann>`,
  :ref:`Wang et al. 2013 <ref-wang>`.

ADNI
----

Data used in this project were obtained from the Alzheimer's Disease
Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) database (adni.loni.usc.edu). Publications
using ADNI data must follow the ADNI data-use agreement and acknowledgement
policy.
